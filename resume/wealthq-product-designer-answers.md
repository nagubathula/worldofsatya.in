# WealthQ Product Designer: screening answers (TalentXO job 7448)

**One product area or 0-to-1 launch you owned end to end?**
Billjot, a billing product for small restaurants. I surveyed 168 restaurants and interviewed 24 in depth, then designed, built, tested, and deployed the first version myself. Owners were held back by cost, staff fear of pressing the wrong button, and habit, so I designed for low cost and simple flows. It went on to INR 2M in revenue. Case study: https://worldofsatya.in/works/billjot

**How often do you talk to users, and one time data changed your design?**
[CONFIRM how often you talk to users today.] On Billjot, testing the first release showed the desktop view worked but the phone view was poor, so I made the waiter's phone the main flow.
